# Tax Compliance & Merchant of Record (MoR) Declaration

**Applicable Product:** MJDM Digital Licenses & Subscriptions  
**Effective Date:** November 16, 2026  

## 1. Merchant of Record Model
MJDM uses an established global Merchant of Record (MoR) service provider (Polar.sh / Lemon Squeezy Inc.) to process international payments, subscriptions, and software license sales.

### Why an MoR?
Operating under a Merchant of Record model ensures that all transactions comply fully with local tax regulations worldwide without requiring customer tax registration hurdles:
- **Reseller Status:** The MoR is the legal entity that sells software licenses to the end customer.
- **Invoicing:** Invoices and receipts are legally issued by the Merchant of Record directly to the consumer or business.
- **Tax Withholding & Remittance:** The MoR is legally responsible for calculating, collecting, reporting, and remitting all applicable:
  - Value Added Tax (VAT) in the European Union (EU VAT OSS) and United Kingdom.
  - Goods and Services Tax (GST) in Australia, Canada, India, and New Zealand.
  - US State Sales and Use Taxes across relevant states.
  - Bangladesh Digital Services Tax (15% VAT on cross-border electronic services where applicable under the National Board of Revenue regulations).

## 3. Refunds & Chargeback Handling
- Customers are entitled to a **14-day unconditional refund**.
- Upon receipt of a refund notification, tax adjustments are credited back to the buyer automatically by the MoR.
